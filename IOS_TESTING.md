# Testing FieldMS on your iPhone

FieldMS is a PWA, so it installs to the home screen straight from Safari — no
App Store, no Apple Developer account, no build step. It runs full screen with
its own icon and no browser chrome.

---

## Install

1. Open **Safari** on the iPhone (it must be Safari — Chrome on iOS cannot
   install a home-screen app)
2. Go to **fieldms.com.au** and sign in
3. Tap the **Share** button (the square with the arrow, bottom centre)
4. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**
5. Tap **Add**

You'll get a FieldMS icon on the home screen. Launch from there, not from
Safari — the two behave differently, and the home-screen one is what you're
testing.

---

## Read this before you start, or you'll chase ghosts

**You will have to sign in again.** An installed PWA gets its own storage,
completely separate from Safari's. Being signed in inside Safari means nothing
to the installed app. This is iOS behaviour, not a bug in FieldMS.

**Xero / MYOB probably won't complete.** OAuth in iOS standalone mode tends to
bounce out to Safari and lose the session on the way back. Neither integration
is actually built yet, so it doesn't matter now — but don't spend an hour
debugging it when you get there. Test integrations in desktop Safari.

**No push notifications.** iOS only delivers them to installed PWAs from 16.4
onward, and FieldMS doesn't implement them at all yet.

**Deploys land immediately.** The service worker is network-first, so you get
the new version on next launch. If something looks stale: delete the icon from
the home screen and re-add it. That clears the PWA's storage completely, which
is also how you reset a broken session.

## What to test

Sign in as the **Voltaic Electrical (demo)** workspace. It has 19 jobs, 12
customers, 30 assets and 16 invoices already in it, so there's real data to
work with rather than empty screens.

### The field app — the part that actually matters on a phone

This is where a technician lives, so it deserves most of your time.

- Sign in as `josh@voltaicelectrical.com.au` (a technician) and check you only
  see **his** jobs — not the customer list, not pricing, not other people's work
- Open a job, work through the form, add a signature
- **Take a photo on a real job** — the camera path is the single thing hardest
  to get right on mobile and impossible to test properly on a desktop
- Ask Fault Finder something real and watch the answer stream in
- Mark a job complete
- Check the bottom nav clears the home indicator (the bar at the very bottom)

### The owner view

- Today screen, Schedule, Jobs, Customers, Money, Compliance
- Check nothing is cut off horizontally and nothing hides behind the status bar
  at the top or the home indicator at the bottom

### Two things worth deliberately breaking

- **Airplane mode.** The app claims to keep working offline. Turn the radio off
  mid-job and see what actually happens. I have not verified this claim.
- **Rotate to landscape.** The manifest asks for portrait, but iOS ignores that
  for home-screen apps, so landscape will happen whether you designed for it or
  not.

## What's already been fixed for this

Both only show up once installed, which is why they survived until now:

- **The icon.** `fieldms-icon.png` is 1890×1417 — not square — and the logo sat
  in the middle of a mostly transparent canvas. iOS squashes a non-square icon
  and composites transparency onto black, so the navy half of the mark was
  vanishing. There are now proper square opaque icons (180/192/512 + maskable),
  generated by `scripts/generate-icons.py`.
- **The status bar.** The app draws edge to edge under the status bar and
  Dynamic Island. Every safe-area rule in the project handled the bottom and
  none handled the top, so both nav bars sat behind the clock. Fixed with
  `.pt-safe` on the two dark top bars.

## Known issues you'll hit that are not iOS's fault

These are open bugs, listed so you don't report them as new:

- **The invite flow is broken.** Adding an employee will not work end to end,
  and onboarding swallows the error silently rather than telling you.
- **Role handling is inconsistent** — `lib/auth.ts` never calls
  `normaliseRole`, so role-gated screens can behave oddly for older accounts.
- **The demo accounts share the password `demo1234`** and are live on a public
  domain. Still needs dealing with.

---

## How to report what you find

Note the **screen**, what you **did**, what you **expected**, and what
**happened** — plus a screenshot (side button + volume up). "The schedule looks
wrong" is much harder to act on than "Schedule, Wednesday column, the 2pm job
overlaps the one above it on a 375px screen".
